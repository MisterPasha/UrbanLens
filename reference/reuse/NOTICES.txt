Owner corrections and source crosswalks describe factual processing decisions,
not independently verified business openings/closures or current operators.
No forecasts, complete business census or footfall estimates are provided.

Format: each public relation has CSV and canonical JSONL; nested CSV cells are
JSON, \N denotes null. Read identifiers as text, preserving leading zeroes.
Use schema.json, areas.geojson and SHA256SUMS to interpret and verify the files.
The exact analytical geometry is stored once in dim_area/areas.geojson;
mart_area joins it by geography_version and area_code.
Excluded businesses contribute aggregate coverage, not public detail records.
Private notes, source contacts and withheld locations are not public data fields.

Corrections/privacy: https://github.com/MisterPasha/UrbanLens/issues
Supply record IDs only; do not post private addresses or other personal details.
Business/register facts can identify individuals. Processing is limited to
location research and source transparency, with no personal enrichment or marketing.
