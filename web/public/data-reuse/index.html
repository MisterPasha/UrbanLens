The ZIP contains CSV and JSONL tables, area boundaries in GeoJSON, schemas, release/source/rule versions, checksums and licence notices. It includes precise eligible venues, non-excluded contextual venue records, area statistics, registered-office companies and observed changes. Context records are not added to competition counts. Private evidence and withheld locations are excluded.
